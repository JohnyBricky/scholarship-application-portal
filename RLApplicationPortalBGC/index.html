Who We Are & What We Do

Our holistic scholarship program equips Senior High School and College scholars in BGC and partner communities to thrive academically and lead with integrity.

01

Educational Assistance

Financial aid that covers tuition and miscellaneous fees so students can focus on finishing strong.

  • School-related expenses: Supplies, uniforms, and project support.
  • Weekly allowances: Support for daily transportation and meals.
  • Course freedom: Scholars choose the SHS strand or college program aligned with their strengths.
02

Character Formation

Good character is equally important as education in preparing students for life.

  • LIFE Groups: Small-group mentoring on leadership, integrity, faith, and excellence with dedicated coaches.
  • Scholar Updates: Monthly community gatherings where scholars share wins, struggles, and mutual encouragement.
03

Leadership Development

Investing in future leaders who will make a lasting impact across every sector of society.

  • Special workshops: Practical life skills, career readiness, and personal well-being sessions.
  • National Scholars’ Conference: Annual summit preparing graduating college scholars for the working world.
